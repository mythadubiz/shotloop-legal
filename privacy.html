Privacy Policy

Shot Loop

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Summary: Shot Loop does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Everything stays on your device. In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Introduction

Shot Loop ("the App") is a party mini-games app for adults, developed by TMD ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App. The App is intended for users aged 18 and over; please play responsibly.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No cookies or similar technologies
  • No microphone, camera, or photo access
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No advertising of any kind

3. Data That Stays On Your Device

The App stores the following information locally (in your device's storage), and it never leaves your device:

  • The players you selected for a party and their round-by-round scores ("drinks")
  • Your chosen intensity mode (Soft, Classic, or Spicy)
  • Your chosen interface language and sound on/off preference
  • Whether you have completed the age confirmation and the intro
  • Your purchase status (whether the Spicy mode is unlocked)

There is no account and no cloud storage — this data is removed when you delete the App.

4. In-App Purchases

The App is free to play and offers a single optional one-time purchase that unlocks the "Spicy" intensity mode. All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store:

  • We never see or receive your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.)
  • Your purchase (the entitlement) is verified on-device via Apple's StoreKit and, if you use Family Sharing, shared through Apple
  • Refunds and payment questions are handled by Apple

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party service used is Apple's own StoreKit / App Store, solely to offer and verify the optional in-app purchase.

6. Age Rating & Responsible Use

Shot Loop is rated 17+ and is designed for adults. It references alcohol in a party-game context. A free no-alcohol "Soft" mode is always available. Please drink responsibly, know your limits, and never drink and drive.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children. It contains no data collection, no external links, no social features, and no chat. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Data Retention & Your Rights

Since we do not collect any data, there is nothing for us to retain. All local data stays on your device until you reset it in the App or delete the App entirely — which removes every trace of your use.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
